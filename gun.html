Machine guns

What is a machine gun?

A machine gun is a type of firearm that is designed to fire bullets in rapid succession from an ammunition belt or magazine. It is capable of sustained automatic fire, making it a powerful weapon in military and law enforcement contexts.

Machine guns are typically used for suppressive fire, providing cover for advancing troops, and defending positions. They can be mounted on vehicles, aircraft, or used by infantry soldiers.

There are various types of machine guns, including light machine guns (LMGs), medium machine guns (MMGs), and heavy machine guns (HMGs), each with different characteristics and uses.
